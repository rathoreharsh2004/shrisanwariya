Established Experience Since 2001

Mr. Dinesh Kumar Dhakad

Managing Director & Owner

Mr. Dinesh Kumar Dhakad is the Managing Director and Owner of Shri Sanwariya Stone, with experience in the sandstone mining industry since 2001.

Since the beginning of his journey, he has been involved in sandstone mining operations, gaining extensive practical knowledge of natural sandstone, mining processes, stone quality and the selection of suitable raw material.

With years of experience in the sandstone industry, his focus has remained on quality, reliable material and consistent standards. His experience in sandstone mining has provided a strong foundation for the company's work in natural stone.

Today, Shri Sanwariya Stone carries forward this experience with a focus on supplying quality sandstone and maintaining reliable standards from the mining stage onward.

Second Generation Leadership

Arman Dhakad

Director – Leadership & Business Development

I am Arman Dhakad, representing the second generation of our family business. Since 2024, I have taken an active role in expanding the business with a modern approach and connecting our products directly with buyers through online platforms.

Our Own Mines & Factory: We operate our own sandstone mining business, Shri Sanwariya Sandstone Supplier, along with our own processing and manufacturing facility, RK Stone Impex. Having our own mining and manufacturing setup allows us to maintain better control over quality, processing, finishing, and supply.

Direct-to-Client Model: We work directly with our clients without unnecessary middlemen, providing reasonable, transparent pricing and delivering genuine value without price markups.

Our Vision: Our goal is to take our sandstone products beyond local markets and connect directly with buyers across India and worldwide. We focus on consistency, quality, commitment, and long-term business relationships.
