07 · Fastener Engineering & Manufacturing

From raw material to finished fastener.

Industry-level manufacturing flow can involve associated manufacturing facilities and manufacturing partners. Shree Pooja Fasteners & Engineering is positioned here as a supplier/wholesaler coordinating suitable products and requirements rather than claiming its own manufacturing facility.

01

Raw Material

Steel, stainless steel and other suitable grades are selected according to product and application requirements.

02

Cold Forming / Heading

Where applicable, wire or bar stock is formed into the fastener head and basic geometry.

03

Thread Formation

Threads can be produced through rolling or machining depending on the component and specification.

04

Heat Treatment

Applicable fasteners may undergo heat treatment to achieve required mechanical properties.

05

Surface Finishing

Finishing or coating may be selected for corrosion resistance, appearance and application requirements.

06

Inspection & Dispatch

Dimensions, threads, appearance, packing and final dispatch requirements are checked.
