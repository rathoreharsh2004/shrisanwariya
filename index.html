ARCHITECTURAL STONE APPLICATION

Flagstones / Crazy Paving.

Random-form irregular paving. For garden paths and organic-layout patios.

Flagstones are random-form fragments sorted from the bed before calibration. The irregular outlines are the character of the format; landscapers fit pieces together on site to produce the dry-stone look of traditional English garden paths. Sold by the crate at a standard coverage rate.

RK STONE IMPEX

Our Manufacturing Facility

Shri Sanwariya Stone is our established sandstone supply business, founded by our father in 2005. Over the years, we have built our experience around sourcing and supplying quality natural sandstone for different projects and customer requirements.

Today, we are taking this business forward with our next-generation approach and our new manufacturing facility, RK Stone Impex.

RK Stone Impex is our new factory where we process and prepare sandstone material for our customers. From block cutting to machine cutting, calibration, polishing and other required finishes, we prepare the material according to project specifications and customer requirements.

Together, Shri Sanwariya Stone and RK Stone Impex represent our journey from an established sandstone supply business to a modern processing and manufacturing setup.

Our focus is on consistent quality, accurate finishing, customized sizes, proper packaging and reliable supply for both Indian and international buyers.
