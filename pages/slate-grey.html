Slate Grey originates from the deep subterranean layers of the Bundi sandstone belt, worked as part of the founding extraction lineage since 1972.

The intense natural pressure of these deep beds produces an unusually dense, hard quartz matrix with a dark charcoal-to-slate grey tone. Highly resistant to water penetration, algae growth, and extreme freezing temperatures, Slate Grey offers an authentic natural alternative to manufactured dark porcelain or granite.

Direct mine extraction and on-site calibrating by Shri Sanwariya Stone Suppliers guarantee precise dimensional tolerances and batch consistency for worldwide export.

Uniform Charcoal Tones · Dense Frost-Proof Quartz

Slate Grey is the preferred stone of modern landscape architects seeking a dramatic dark palette without the synthetic sheen of porcelain or the high maintenance of imported slate.

Its uniform charcoal tone deepens elegantly when wet and contrasts strikingly with lush green lawn borders, light timber accents, and rendered white architectural walls.

Key Advantages

Consistent Dark Aesthetic: Reliable deep charcoal tone across large paving projects.

Ultra-Low Porosity: Less than 1.1% absorption prevents water staining and freeze-thaw damage.
