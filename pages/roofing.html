FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions buyers ask about Stone Roofing Slates

What is the recommended roof pitch for natural sandstone slates? ▾

We recommend a minimum roof pitch of 25° to 30° for standard double-lap installation with a 75 mm to 100 mm headlap depending on local wind and rain exposure ratings. For steeper pitches (45°+), diminishing courses provide superior drainage and traditional heritage appearance.

Are the fixing holes pre-drilled or pre-punched at the factory? ▾

Yes. All our export roof slates come factory pre-drilled with clean, countersunk fixing holes positioned at approximately 400 mm to 410 mm from the tail edge (or customized to your batten gauge schedule). This eliminates on-site stone cracking and dramatically accelerates installation time.

Can you supply diminishing courses for traditional heritage roofs? ▾

Absolutely. We specialize in bespoke diminishing courses schedules, providing graduated lengths (e.g. 600 mm at the eaves down to 250 mm near the ridge) with matching diminishing widths and thicknesses to faithfully recreate Cotswold and traditional European roofing styles.

How does Indian quartzitic sandstone compare to Welsh or Spanish slate? ▾

Our Indian quartzitic sandstone offers higher flexural impact strength and thicker, more substantial cleft character (18–22mm vs 5–7mm metamorphic slates), delivering unmatched thermal insulation, authentic historic rustic charm, and excellent freeze-thaw durability at substantially more competitive quarry-direct pricing.
