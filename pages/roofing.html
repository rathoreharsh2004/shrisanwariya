Questions buyers ask about Natural Stone Roofing

How do natural sandstone slates compare with Welsh or Spanish slate? ▾
Our Indian quartzitic sandstone slates offer exceptional density, greater resistance to atmospheric acid rain, and warmer earthy color tones compared to conventional dark grey metamorphic slates, providing a distinctive rustic heritage look.
Are the slates supplied pre-holed for copper nail fixing? ▾
Yes, all slates can be precision pre-drilled with two countersunk nail holes positioned for head-lap or center-lap fixing depending on your specified roof pitch and batten gauge.
What pitch of roof is suitable for sandstone slates? ▾
Our slates are routinely installed on roof pitches ranging from 25° to 65°. For lower pitches (between 25° and 30°), an increased headlap (e.g. 100mm) is specified along with a breathable underlay membrane.
