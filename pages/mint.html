Mined from the renowned stone-bearing layers of the Bijolia belt in Rajasthan, Mint sandstone is globally cherished for its serene aesthetic and unique mineral characteristics.

The stone features a luminous pale celadon and soft mint foundation accented by subtle ochre banding. Distinctively, select beds exhibit exquisite dendritic manganese dendrites—delicate, fern-like crystal patterns formed naturally over millions of years within the rock fissures.

Its fine-to-medium grained quartzitic composition gives it high compressive strength, exceptional frost resistance, and a cooling surface temperature that remains pleasant under bare feet even during high summer.

Architectural Advantage

Solar Reflectance & Natural Fossils

Mint's pale reflective tones retain substantially less solar heat than darker flags, while natural dendritic crystallization provides botanical interest that no artificial porcelain tile can replicate.
