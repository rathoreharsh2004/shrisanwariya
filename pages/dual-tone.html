York Grey represents one of the most remarkable natural geological phenomena in the Vindhyan sandstone system, mined from specialized transition beds in the Bijolia belt of Rajasthan.

At the contact boundary where silver-grey quartzitic silts met ferric ochre sand layers, ancient sedimentation fused the two distinct mineral beds into a monolithic stone stratum. When split along natural bed cleavage planes, each slab reveals a dramatic diagonal or undulating interface dividing cool steel-grey from warm golden-tan.

Because the two tones are structurally integral—fused at a molecular level—there is zero seam weakness. York Grey provides landscape architects with an artistic, bi-chromatic paving solution within a single, highly durable natural material.
