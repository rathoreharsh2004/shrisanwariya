Raveena is mined from the specialized mineral-rich strata of the Khokri block in Bhilwara District, extracted continuously since the 1990s.

Geologically distinctive, the Khokri deposit is enriched with light manganese and iron oxide wave patterns that create delicate pastel ribbons across each slab. The soft rose-tinted purples, warm creams, and pale celadon mints reflect sunlight radiantly without absorbing excessive summer heat.

Direct quarry-to-crate processing through Shri Sanwariya Stone Suppliers ensures careful color sorting so each container displays an elegantly balanced pastel harmony.
