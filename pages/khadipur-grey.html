Khadipur Grey is quarried from the Khadipur mining block, part of the renowned Bijolia sandstone belt in Bhilwara District, Rajasthan — one of India's most established natural stone producing regions.

The Bijolia belt is known for its diverse range of sandstone colours and reliable consistency of material. Khadipur Grey is distinguished by its characteristically cool grey palette, consistent grain structure, and dependable layering — making it suitable for both flat paving and dimensional stone applications.

Stone Character

Khadipur Grey
as a Natural Alternative

Cool · Consistent · Versatile

Khadipur Grey is frequently specified as a natural alternative to concrete and porcelain grey, offering a material that is both visually refined and durable under outdoor conditions.

Its cool, restrained tone pairs naturally with modern architectural finishes — steel, glass, light render — while remaining equally at home in traditional garden and courtyard settings.

Why Khadipur Grey

The stone's consistent mid-grey colour profile makes it a reliable choice for large-area paving where uniformity matters — patios, driveways, pool surrounds, and commercial plazas.

Unlike manufactured alternatives, Khadipur Grey retains a natural anti-slip texture on its riven face, offering both aesthetic and practical benefits without additional surface treatments.
