Questions buyers ask about Steps

What is the difference between solid steps and tread-riser kits? ▾
Solid steps are single monoblock pieces of stone (typically 50mm thick) that provide an imposing, monolithic aesthetic. Tread-and-riser sets consist of a 25-30mm thick horizontal tread paired with a matching 150mm vertical riser panel to clad existing concrete substructures.
Can you fabricate custom radial or curved step treads? ▾
Yes, we manufacture bespoke semi-circular and curved radius steps based on architect plans, complete with pre-numbered pieces for puzzle-like laying on site.
How do you manage container weight with heavy 50mm steps? ▾
Because 50mm stone is dense, we calculate crate weights precisely to ensure containers reach maximum permissible payload (~27-28 tons) without exceeding axle weight limits in your destination port.
