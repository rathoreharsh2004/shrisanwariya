FAQS

Questions buyers ask about Sandstone Steps

Can you supply matching step treads and paving in the same color tone? ▼
Yes, we quarry and fabricate our sandstone step treads, risers and matching patio pavers from identical sandstone deposits, ensuring uniform color and finish across the entire project.
Are custom step thicknesses and edging options available? ▼
Absolutely. We manufacture step treads from 30mm, 40mm, and 50mm calibrated thickness up to solid 150mm thick block steps. Edge profiles include full bullnose, half bullnose, hand chiseled rockface, and modern sawn chamfered.
What is the slip resistance rating of your stone step treads? ▼
Our natural split, shotblasted, flamed, and bush-hammered step surfaces provide high wet slip resistance ratings (PTV > 45 / R11+), exceeding British and European standards for external pedestrian stairs.
How are step treads and heavy block steps packed for export? ▼
Solid block steps and treads are separated with protective high-density foam sheets, shrink-wrapped, steel-banded, and securely crated onto certified ISPM-15 heat-treated heavy pallets designed for container forklift unloading.
