The People Behind the Stone

Established Experience Since 2001

Mr. Dinesh Kumar Dhaakad

Managing Director & Owner

Mr. Dinesh Kumar Dhaakad is the Managing Director and Owner of Shri Sanwariya Stone, with experience in the sandstone mining industry since 2001.

Since the beginning of his journey, he has been involved in sandstone mining operations, gaining extensive practical knowledge of natural sandstone, mining processes, stone quality and the selection of suitable raw material.

With years of experience in the sandstone industry, his focus has remained on quality, reliable material and consistent standards. His experience in sandstone mining has provided a strong foundation for the company's work in natural stone.

Today, Shri Sanwariya Stone carries forward this experience with a focus on supplying quality sandstone and maintaining reliable standards from the mining stage onward.

"The quality of a stone speaks for itself — our job is simply to deliver that quality to every client, every time."
Second Generation · Since 2024

Arman Dhakar

Director – Leadership & Business Development

I am Arman Dhakar, representing the second generation of our family business. Since 2024, I have taken an active role in expanding the business with a modern approach and connecting our products directly with buyers through online platforms.

Our Own Mines & Factory

We operate our own sandstone mining business, Shri Sanwariya Sandstone Supplier, along with our own processing and manufacturing facility, RK Stone Impex. Having our own mining and manufacturing setup allows us to maintain better control over quality, processing, finishing, and supply.

Direct-to-Client Model

We believe in working directly with our clients without unnecessary middlemen. This helps us provide reasonable and transparent pricing and deliver a genuine value-for-money product without unnecessary price markups.

Our Vision

Our goal is to take our sandstone products beyond local markets and connect directly with buyers across India and worldwide. We focus on consistency, quality, commitment, and long-term business relationships. Our aim is to grow with our clients and build a trusted name in the sandstone industry.

"Building on our family legacy since 2005, while creating a stronger and more connected future for RK Sandstone."
