FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions buyers ask about Sandstone Fire Pits

Can fire pits be customized to project-specific dimensions? ▾

Yes! We specialize in tailor-made stone masonry. We produce square fire pits from 600 mm up to 1800 mm, circular fire rings up to 2.5 m diameter, and custom radial benches to match your landscape architecture drawings with bespoke coping edge profiles.

Do you supply matching paving stones or stone circles for fire pits? ▾

Absolutely. We supply matching natural sandstone patio pavers, 2.0m to 4.0m radial circular paving kits, cobblestone borders, and pool copings in matching colors like Kandla Grey, Raj Green, Teakwood, and Mandana Red for a cohesive outdoor living space.

Are natural sandstone fire pits safe for high-heat wood burning? ▾

Yes. Our dense Indian quartzitic sandstone has high thermal stability. For wood-burning setups, we recommend kit configurations that include heavy-gauge steel fire bowl inserts or firebrick liners to evenly distribute heat and protect stone longevity.

Can these fire pits accommodate propane (LPG) or natural gas burners? ▾

Yes. Our kits can be factory pre-drilled with discreet gas line conduit holes and control panel recesses to seamlessly accommodate modern electronic ignition gas burners, lava rock, or fire glass.
