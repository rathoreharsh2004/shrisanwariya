Questions buyers ask about Sandstone Pavers

Can you supply mixed-size patio project packs? ▾
Yes! We specialize in standard 4-size mixed patio packs (e.g., 900x600, 600x600, 600x290, 290x290 mm) calibrated at uniform 22mm, packaged for quick direct laying on site without pre-sorting.
What is the difference between calibrated and hand-cut sandstone paving? ▾
Calibrated paving slabs are run through mechanical calibrator saws on the bottom to achieve an exact, uniform thickness (typically 22mm). This significantly speeds up installation. Hand-cut paving retains traditional natural cleft variations on both faces.
How does Indian sandstone perform in freezing winters? ▾
Our hard quartzitic sandstones exhibit ultra-low water absorption (< 1.5%) and high flexural density, making them fully resistant to frost weathering and freeze-thaw cycles across UK and European climates.
