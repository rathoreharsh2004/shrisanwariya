FAQ

Questions buyers ask about Sandstone Pavers

Is it preferable to order the calibrated gauge of 22mm or uncalibrated? ▼
Calibrated 22mm ensures uniform bed depth, speeding up installation and providing perfectly level paving surfaces for residential patios and commercial walkways.
Which finish offers maximum slip-resistance around swimming pools? ▼
Natural split (riven) and shotblasted finishes provide exceptional slip resistance even when wet (tested R11+), making them the safest choice for pool copings and surrounds.
What is included in a standard 4-size mixed patio pack? ▼
Standard 4-size mixed patio packs typically comprise 900x600 mm, 600x600 mm, 600x290 mm, and 290x290 mm calibrated pieces, designed to repeat in random or modular patterns across ~15.3 m² or ~19 m² per crate.
