FAQS

Questions buyers ask about Sandstone Cobbles & Setts

Are your cobbles suitable for heavy vehicular and commercial traffic? ▼
Yes. When laid on a suitable mortar or concrete sub-base, our 50mm and 60mm thick setts easily withstand heavy driveway and passenger SUV traffic. For commercial plazas, roadway crossings, and bus lanes, we manufacture heavy-duty 80mm and 100mm thick setts.
Can cobbles be supplied in pre-formed fan or circular patterns? ▼
Yes, we provide individual radius-cut tapered setts for authentic on-site radial paving and concentric circles, as well as pre-assembled mesh-backed fan segments for rapid commercial installation.
What colors and stone types are available in setts? ▼
We quarry and process setts in Kandla Grey, Raj Green, Sagar Black, Mandana Red, Autumn Brown, and Camel Dust in hand-cut rustic, tumbled antique, or sawn shotblasted finishes.
