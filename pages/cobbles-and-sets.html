Questions buyers ask about Cobbles & Sets

What thickness of cobble sett is required for driveways? ▾
For domestic passenger car driveways, a 40-50mm thick sett installed on a compacted sub-base is sufficient. For heavy SUVs, commercial delivery access, or civic roads, we recommend 60-80mm thickness for maximum interlock and shear resistance.
How many 100x100mm setts are there in a square meter? ▾
Allowing for standard 8-10mm mortar or resin joint gaps, approximately 82 to 86 pieces of 100x100mm cobbles cover one square meter of laid area.
Can cobbles be bundled with slabs in container orders? ▾
Yes, many clients add 2 to 4 crates of matching 100x100mm or 100x200mm cobbles to a container of paving slabs to serve as complementary driveway aprons and patio edging borders.
