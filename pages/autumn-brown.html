Nayanagar Quarry,

Quarried from the historic Nayanagar block within the Bijolia tehsil of Bhilwara District, Autumn Brown has been one of India’s most prized warm-toned sandstones since the 1980s.

Rich iron-oxide mineral deposits give this stone its signature warm brown, chocolate, and tan palette. Its compact sedimentary grain delivers outstanding structural strength, weather resilience, and natural slip resistance that matures gracefully over decades.

Direct quarry-to-port processing through Shri Sanwariya Stone Suppliers ensures calibrated thicknesses, clean hand-cut or sawn edges, and strict batch color matching.
