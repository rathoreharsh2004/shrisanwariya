Sourced directly from our family's proprietary quarrying block in Garda village, Bundi District. Unlike third-party traders, Shri Sanwariya Stone operates its own extraction setup at the Garda block, ensuring primary control over color sorting, natural cleft bedding, and dimensional accuracy.
