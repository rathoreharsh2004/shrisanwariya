Quarried from the natural variegated beds across the Bijolia sandstone belt, Raj Blend captures the full spectrum of the regional geology in every batch.

Unlike single-color stones, Raj Blend is cherished for its organic tonal tapestry. No two slabs are identical, displaying warm ochres, soft dove greys, gentle purplish-browns, and rich autumnal highlights that create warmth and timeless prestige across large patio and landscape areas.

Shri Sanwariya Stone Suppliers carefully balances the multi-tone ratio in every calibrated crate pack, guaranteeing a harmonious blend upon installation.

Why Raj Blend is the

Warm · Authentic · Forgiving Surface

Raj Blend is often described as the most natural-looking stone available. Because it contains both warm and cool mineral undertones, it harmonizes effortlessly with almost any exterior wall color, garden border, or landscaping style.

Its variegated surface is also exceptionally forgiving with outdoor dust, leaves, and daily weathering, maintaining an attractive appearance year-round with minimal maintenance.

Key Advantages

Effortless Pairing: Blends warm terracotta tones with cool greys to complement any architectural style.

Natural Slip Safety: Traditional hand-cleft texture gives certified slip resistance under foot.

Proven Weathering: Resists severe freeze-thaw cycles without delaminating or spalling.
