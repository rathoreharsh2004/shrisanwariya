FAQS

Questions buyers ask about Copings

Do you provide drip grooves on your coping stones? ▼
Yes, we can pre-cut drip grooves (channels) on the underside of coping stones (usually 10-15mm from the front or back edge) to stop rainwater tracking back and staining the masonry wall below.
Can copings stones be matched with the same paving color? ▼
Absolutely. We quarry and process our copings directly from the same sandstone beds (such as Kandla Grey, Autumn Brown, Raj Blend, and Slate Grey) ensuring seamless color and textural harmony across your entire patio, steps, and boundary walls.
What edge profiles do you offer for swimming pools? ▼
For swimming pools, Full Bullnose (smooth 180° round edge) and Half Bullnose (smooth top radius with square bottom) are the most popular to prevent sharp contact. Drop-down rebate copings (L-shape profile) are also custom manufactured for seamless waterline finishes.
Can you supply curved and bespoke corner coping units? ▼
Yes. We manufacture internal and external 90-degree corner units, as well as radius curves (1.5m, 2m, and 3m radii) with matching edge profiles to accommodate Roman end pools and circular wall designs.
