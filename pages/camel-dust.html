Natural Cleft · Camel Dust

Source

Bhootii Block,
Bhilwara District

Directly mined from the prolific Bhootii block within the Bijolia tehsil of Bhilwara district, Camel Dust has been quarried and supplied by our family operations since the 1990s.

Geologically composed of deeply compacted Vindhyan quartzites, Camel Dust exhibits a natural desert-sand spectrum—warm golden caramel, toasted wheat, and subtle fawn-tan ripples that echo the windswept dunes of the Thar.

Its natural riven cleft provides an intrinsically slip-resistant surface with outstanding mechanical density. Because the stone is naturally resistant to acid rain, frost spalling, and environmental pollutants, it preserves its sunny, earthen charm indefinitely.
