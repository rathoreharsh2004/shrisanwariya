Flagstones / Crazy Paving

Random-form irregular paving. For garden paths, courtyards, pool surrounds, and organic-layout patios.

Flagstones are random-form fragments sorted from the quarry bed before calibration. The irregular outlines are the authentic character of the format; landscapers fit pieces together on site to produce the dry-stone look of traditional English garden paths, rustic Mediterranean terraces, and freeform organic patios. Every piece features a natural split non-slip surface, dense frost-resistant sandstone composition, and uniform calibrated depth for efficient bed installation. Sold by the crate at a standard coverage rate.

FAQS

Questions buyers ask about Crazy / Flagstone Paving

How is coverage calculated for Crazy / Flagstone Paving? ▼
Flagstone crazy paving is sold by square meter coverage or by the crate. Each crate typically provides 15 to 20 square meters of coverage depending on stone thickness and installation joint width (usually 10mm to 30mm joints).
Are your crazy paving flagstones calibrated? ▼
Yes. We provide precision calibrated flagstones at 20mm, 22mm, 25mm, 30mm, and 35mm thicknesses. Calibrating the underside allows contractors to lay irregular stones much faster on a uniform bed depth without uneven rocking.
Is crazy paving slip-resistant for swimming pool surrounds? ▼
Extremely slip-resistant. The natural riven cleft texture of Indian sandstone provides exceptional wet grip underfoot, making it one of the most popular choices worldwide for swimming pool copings, beach entries, and wet garden paths.
What stone colors can be supplied in crazy paving? ▼
All 15 authentic quarry colors are available, including Kandla Grey, Autumn Brown, Raj Blend, Mandana Red, Mint Fossil, Raveena, Buff, Camel Dust, and White Quartzite.
